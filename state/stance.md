## The event

Saturday. Targets — a **government facility in northern Kuwait** and civilian
vehicles of a private company on **Bubiyan Island**. Material damage from

soil of a GCC state in this war (day 153). Kuwait hosts Camp Arifjan and Ali
Al Salem. Bubiyan sits at the head of the Gulf beside the Iraqi border. This
converts "US energy assets across the Middle East" from an Iranian threat into
an Iranian action, and it widens the war from Iran–Israel/US to Iran–GCC.

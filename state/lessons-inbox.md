- 2026-11-10 (Tue brief): the CNBC `@GC.1` "Prev Close" field read at 03:31Z Tue was 4,114.50 = the 18:30Z COMEX settlement (GC 18:50Z row 4,114.0), NOT the Globex 22:00Z close (GC 22:00Z row 4,149.5; GC_CLOSE 21:30Z 4,147.5) - a 35-pt gap after a late-session move - while on 2 Nov the same field matched the Friday Globex row. Rule 17 states the CNBC prev close IS the Globex close; it is not on a late-move day. Suggested rule: a settle claim names "the CNBC prev-close field as read" as its basis and the brief logs the 22:00Z GC row beside it; a line within ~35 pts of either reading is inside the readings' gap (rule 9 letter-only); the first deepdive after a late-move day checks which print CNBC rolls to and when.
- 2026-11-10 (Tue brief): the dollar leg was written one-directional ("a third DXY >=+0.35 close with gold DOWN names the dollar"); Monday's third session was DXY -0.63 with gold UP and had to be counted by judgment. Rule 11 sign tests count both signs of the driver's move. Suggested rule: a reaction-function count is written symmetric ("a DXY move >=0.35 either way with gold in the inverse sign") and names which sessions it has so far, so the next brief can count without re-deriving.
- 2026-11-10 (11:00Z scan): the brief's Waller marker said "Istanbul"; CNBC placed the speech in London. Harmless here, but rule-24 venue checks drive whether a Fed slot is booked at all. Suggested rule: a Fed-speaker marker names the venue only from the Board/regional index or the relay text, never from a calendar aggregator's guess; write "venue unverified" otherwise.
- 2026-11-11 (Wed brief): the Tue stance counted f(2y) twice for one day - "1 of 3 (restarted Tue 11:00Z on the Waller leg)" and "the print session is the second count" - though both are the same settle. Rule 11 counts sessions on the settle; an intraday leg and that day's close are one count. Suggested rule: a reaction-function count is written per SETTLE session with the dates listed ("Tue 10 Nov: 1"), never per intraday leg; a scan may mark "in progress", never increment.

## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact — and a posture fact the
   same day.** No geopolitical claim enters `stance.md` without an extracted
   source saying who did what to whom, since when. A US-force actor paired
   with withdraw / deploy / carrier / munitions is a posture fact the brief
   extracts in its own run; "reports" with no Tehran or Washington statement
   stay unconfirmed; a principal's "we can do that in 10 minutes" is intent.
   — evidence: 31 Jul Hormuz inverted; 30 Sep–1 Oct Iraq withdrawal
   unextracted through eight runs; 15 Oct IRGC "reports" held (`23a83bcb`
   HIT); 22–24 Oct strike relays held as posture (`df0e8755` HIT); 31 Oct
   Trump "one or two sites" and Khamenei's doctrine held as intent/posture.

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class named
   — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Falsifier prints → kill
   the call in the same run; a move prices the call → mark it realized; an
   intent statement neither kills nor scores a claim written on the act.
   — evidence: 1, 5, 31 Aug kills; `c4ef5615` scored as written; `a270f3f1`
   drifted to UNCLEAR; `b4938377` held open through Trump's 31 Oct intent.

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — per session, not
   per regime — and "none of the above" is the positioning read.** A setter
   named last week is re-earned this week; two price-setter sessions in a
   row demote the standing function to a hypothesis; oil sets the size of a
   session the 2y signs; a de-grossing sells gold with every input positive.
   — evidence: fifteen oil-UP sessions in Sep closed gold flat/down; 28
   Sep–2 Oct f(2y) 5/5; 22 Oct 2y −18.8, gold −2.2%; 29–30 Oct 2y −7.9 /
   −6.4 and gold −20 / −146.5 — f(2y) demoted after two margin-book
   sessions, the sixth test of 4000 failed on no input.

4. **Chokepoint and shipping claims go through the maritime trade press —
   gcaptain search (todo-005), Maritime Executive, Gulf News/AFP third for
   the Sat–Sun time-late check.** Date-check mandatory on `?s=` results; a
   warning-count claim says "as carried by" the named outlets.
   — evidence: 19 Aug Wafa; 2 Oct second Hormuz hit ~10h late via AFP; 21
   Oct Al-Faw via Mehr and MarEx (`55f22cae` MISS); 30 Oct limpet (UKMTO
   187-26) via MarEx at the 09:00Z scan, gcaptain 15:16Z.

5. **A cap against the controlling impulse needs three conditions and tops
   at 0.6 even then.** Impulse against the level AND no Fed speaker in the
   window AND the in-window data consensus skewed with the cap; else 0.5.
   Inside one expected range after an event-driven day, or inside an
   enforcement campaign with <1 ATR of room, 0.5. Prefer the hold-side claim.
   — evidence: `a3ad30b1` hit at 0.6 under all three; `f8b7b030` `65cd057e`
   `09cb81a7` missed; against-impulse 4/17 lifetime, none written this week.

6. **"Only X can cause Y" must enumerate and reject the third channel —
   named per claim family.** Fiscal/debasement for gold; tone without
   guidance for Fed pricing; the other central bank for FX pillars; the oil
   leg for "hot print → gold down"; the credit leg for "hot print → 2y up"
   and "oil up → 2y up"; the long end for "Fed hold → gold". Can't reject
   it, drop "only."
   — evidence: tone without guidance 5/5 for ≥10-pt repricings (28 Oct: a
   hawkish statement for 30 min, "it's worked some" reversed the 2y); 15
   Oct core PPI 0.7 with the 2y −5bp (`7f2116ce` MISS); 22 Oct Brent +4%,
   2y −19bp; 28 Oct 2y −0.2, 10y +5.2, gold −23 on the long end.

7. **A conditional's trigger is sized like a target or is a scheduled gate;
   a chain test needs a scheduled driver that moves the CURVE, not one
   tenor; a band on a chain input is sized on that input's MEASURED range.**
   A trigger not expected inside the window at ≥1× its own ATR-days becomes
   a band or a sign claim on the chain's input; the form that never goes
   UNCLEAR is the gate certain to print, scored in-session from one named
   basis; never a chain test for the session before a tier-1 print (rule
   20). On an auction gate the driver counts only if the 2y and 10y close
   in the same sign by ≥0.5 ATR each; a 2y-up / 10y-down session is "supply
   split" and UNCLEAR — or the gate claim is written on the auction tail
   itself (grade ≤C → 2y close above WI). A band on the 2y, Brent, USDJPY
   or the 30y is sized on its 10-session realized range with ≥2× the
   window's expected range on the side of any live or dormant stance leg;
   otherwise the claim waits for the gate.
   — evidence: three conditionals UNCLEAR by construction (Sep–Oct);
   auction gates 3/3; `ddf6e395` offside in 14h on "~5bp", HIT inside the
   measured range; `db5c80e2` MISS — the C− 2y auction lifted the 2y +2.5
   while the 10y/30y fell and gold settled +28.4; `7d5c839b` HIT.

   wrong-reason hit is discounted; a hit inside the measurement error or a
   sign claim at zero distance from its comparator carries nothing; a
   trigger missing by <0.5 ATR with the target running against is
   "substance against", counted in the family's calibration.
   — evidence: `62c34dbd` letter-only; `1e9e6e4a` on a 0bp line; `51f5086d`
   held by 17.9 with the tick through by 0.9; `6754924a` row 3898.9 held

11. **Pattern extrapolation needs a live transmission mechanism; a horizon
    >14 days re-verifies at the midpoint; a sign test counts only sessions
    on which its driver moved, settle first, and logs a split by TIME.** A
    reaction function enters the stance as fact only after three sessions in
    the same direction with the driver moving ≥1 daily ATR. The test counts
    a session only when the driver moves ≥0.5 of its own ATR (2y ≥2bp, 10y
    ≥4bp, DXY ≥0.2), names as driver the instrument that moved ≥1 ATR on the
    sessions gold moved ≥0.5 ATR, is demoted by two flat-driver sessions in
    five or two price-setter sessions in a row (rule 3), and on an auction
    day counts only a parallel move (rule 7). When the 2y's close move and
    its ~13:30 ET level differ in sign the session is "timing split — chain
    post-settle": counted on the ROW with the settle noted; a chain-test
    claim names the 13:30 ET 2y as a second comparator.
    — evidence: `9a757c1a` `71096269` `306d7862` misses; `31da0c7d` HIT;
    chain tests since 11 Oct 1/2/2; f(2y) 12/19 on the settle since 28 Sep,
    the last four counted sessions (26, 27, 29, 30 Oct) against; 21 Oct
    timing split; 22 Oct the de-grossing exception.

12. **Operator confirmation stands alone; an act is scored by its incident
    date; adversary claims are never a trigger; a negative is a class
    negative only on an unreached class; a weapon class is ONE weapon; no
    location negative inside a reached class while the count is rising.**
    Incident and attribution claims score on UKMTO/operator/host-state/
    US-official statements only. A tactic the actor has used before in the
    same theatre, with a reported attempt this cycle, is a REACHED class
    (0.5 cap) even on a clean operator record; the structural negative goes
    on the genuinely new class (a laid mine on the operator record, a
    sinking, a US-flagged hull, a terminal suspension). While the UKMTO
    count is rising the brief writes the next-class negative, never a
    location inside a reached class. A hull/mine negative is scored HIT only
    after the UKMTO weekly (via gcaptain), gcaptain and Gulf News time-late
    checks for the last two window days; a later confirmation re-opens the
    score at the retro.

## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact.** No geopolitical claim
   enters `stance.md` without extracting a source and confirming *who is
   doing what to whom* and *since when* — the two fields headlines most
   often invert or omit.
   — evidence: 31 Jul Hormuz who-blockades-whom inverted; 5 Sep Mehr-only
   Kharg strike held single-source until CENTCOM; 11 Sep Hormuz-plan report
   carried as unsourced while the scans took the on-record GCC response.

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class
   named — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Kinetic triggers
   name the actor and the target class (a hull at the Kharg anchorage is a
   hull, not territory). When the falsifier prints, kill the call in the
   same run; when a move prices a call, mark it realized the same run.
   — evidence: 1 Aug, 5 Aug, 31 Aug kills; 6 Sep flip line rewritten after
   the 5 Sep scan could not classify a tanker strike; `c4ef5615` scored as
   written on the Derya (hull, 10 Sep).

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — not the
   direction.** Repeated non-response to a textbook driver identifies the
   marginal desk; "rates desk in control" read as "range-bound" is the error.
   — evidence: `f7d39052` `394dfea7` `031e3470` caps (Aug–2 Sep) vs
   `52e1b990` `54b6127f` (settle lower with the chain named) hits.

4. **Chokepoint and shipping claims go through the maritime trade press —
   and, while todo-005 is open, through gcaptain search, not just ingest.**
   Date-check mandatory on `?s=` results.
   — evidence: 2 Aug who-restricts-Hormuz; 19 Aug RSS missed Wafa (todo-005);
   `bb86ccc9` 24 Aug; `f04926f1` `2fbc22dd` scored on gcaptain sweeps 13 Sep.

5. **A cap is written against the controlling impulse only under three
   conditions, and a gold cap tops at 0.6 even then.** The impulse points
   against the level AND no Fed speaker or governor sits in the window AND
   the in-window data consensus skews with the cap; else the ceiling is 0.5.
   Any cap inside one expected range (ATR × √days) after an event-driven
   day, or inside an active enforcement campaign with <1 ATR of room, tops
   at 0.5; a "premium, not barrels" cap names the enforcement change as its
   first breaker. Prefer the falsifiable hold-side claim.
   — evidence: `a3ad30b1` hit at 0.6 under all three (11 Sep); `f8b7b030`
   (65 pts on ATR 110 after −1.9%), `65cd057e` (~1% room, turn-backs →
   disabling fire), `09cb81a7` (FOMC inside) missed; gold caps 1/8 on
   substance since 5 Aug; every floor/hold 12/12 in the 7–20 Sep fortnight.

6. **"Only X can cause Y" must enumerate and reject the third channel —
   named per claim family.** Fiscal/debasement for gold; *tone without
   guidance* for Fed pricing; the *other* central bank for FX pillars; the
   *oil leg* for every "hot print → gold down" line. Can't reject it, drop
   "only."
   — evidence: `394dfea7` `98bbfd33` `b6450c7a`; tone without guidance 4/4
   for ≥10-pt Fed repricings (16 Sep: no guidance sentence, presser moved
   Oct 38 → 55–58); 11 Sep core 0.318 fired the flip, gold +1.4% on oil.

7. **Announcement-timing, framing, and "scheduled event happens" claims cap
   at ≤0.5 unless anchored to a host- or neutral-published schedule.** A
   participant's announcement is not a schedule; coverage volume never
   raises it.
   — evidence: 0/7 lifetime (`7fcb559d` `fb90abae` `df9a603b` `cd49c313`
   `55412a56` `8babc60f` `6c6ed87f` — Baghaei-only anchor, Oman postponed).

8. **No-incident claims require a live feed, and a falsifier the feed can
   resolve.** If UKMTO is dark, scope to operator/press-confirmed events;
   the text states whether "fired upon" counts and names the source that
   resolves position, or drops the qualifier; an infrastructure claim names
   the whole chain it argues about, or states node scope and why the rest
   is out.
   — evidence: `aba1ae09` miss (UKMTO dark); `6b3ad492` UNCLEAR ("subject
   to disabling fire", no hit, no position); `f04926f1` node held while the
   chain was cut inland; `81e84f0c` `47d2085d` scoped correctly, hit.

9. **Separate trigger from target in compound claims.** A level-plus-
   mechanism claim is scored on both; a wrong-reason hit is discounted, not
   banked; a hit inside the measurement error carries no information.
   — evidence: `7bca8d8f` `6f772055` (Aug); floors `746afbe2` `cdb1709a`
   `66db8e8e` `63bf960c` hit on distance with the named buyer unobserved;
   `cdb1709a` the first whose breaker fired and failed; `b06eaec0` by 1.6 pts.

10. **Skeptic's edge is a floor: structural-negative claims start at 0.8.**
    "No co-confirmed text / no strike on class X / regime persists" backed by
    on-record positions is written at 0.8+; lower requires naming the
    scheduled gate. The brief's 0.7 ceiling (todo-007) still blocks it —
    name the compliant floor in the text.
    — evidence: 50/50 across 3 Aug–19 Sep; 14/14 at the ceiling 7–20 Sep.

11. **Pattern extrapolation needs a live transmission mechanism — and a
    horizon >14 days re-verifies it at the midpoint.** Name what carries the
    pattern forward (else cap 0.6); kill at the midpoint if it died.
    — evidence: `9a757c1a` `71096269` misses; `a64b23c1` `bb86ccc9` hits at
    0.8; `951d8286` (29 days) missed by zero margin; `ec71ce01` (0.8, 41
    days, one payroll print, midpoint on Warsh's Jackson Hole speech, never
    checked) missed on a delivered hike.

12. **Operator confirmation stands alone; namelessness downgrades detail,
    not the event; adversary claims are never a trigger.** Incident-count
    claims score on UKMTO/operator/host-state confirmation only.
    — evidence: `0aaec9a2` hit, `71096269` miss (Aug); `c2eafbf8` `bd825267`
    wrote "or IRGC-claimed" and scored hit on a CENTCOM-denied claim — two
    hits carrying zero information; `71bd8ddb` hit right-reason.

13. **Status claims come from running the check — levels, events,
    infrastructure, trajectories and feed gaps alike.** A trajectory call
    sweeps the claim's whole window from `created_at` and cites the earliest
    qualifying event; a component or feed reported missing must have been
    queried, missing, in the same run.
    — evidence: calendar "outage" ×4 retros, "`bars` empty" (6 Sep); 6 Sep
    "miss trajectory" on `bd825267`/`c2eafbf8` from the latest event only;
    11 Sep "no feed carried the Hormuz-plan report" — investing_commodities
    had it at 01:01Z, twelve hours earlier.

14. **Data-event runs: schedule off the US print (print+45), never the
    foreign presser; read the print post before the fronts; name the
    component.** The investinglive print post carries the odds sentence
    first, actionforex's front at ~print+50 second; "no relay" is declared
    only after both. A hot/soft verdict names the largest single component
    and the ex-that number before the odds sentence. A run that has written
    state never exits `empty` (todo-180).
    — evidence: 12 Aug CPI at print+15 empty; 4 Sep NFP `empty` after writes;
    #53 (ECB moved GC 2 pts, PPI 30; odds at print+15 in the post); #26
    (print+45 worked; wireless +5.9% was 0.1pp of core).

15. **Date agency copy from its own dateline; trust extracts only as of the
    freshness header; check the extracted title against the headline.**
    — evidence: 16 Aug AJ stale index; 19–21 Aug Saree via Mehr; 6 Sep Gulf
    News mis-serve (todo-181); 14 Sep "wounded USAF officer" was an April
    shoot-down interview.

16. **A scheduled-date anchor needs primary verification ≤7 days old; a
    diplomatic-event anchor cites the host or venue state.** Participant-only
    announcements schedule a scan marker, not a deepdive; the Saturday brief
    re-verifies the coming week; the 21:00Z Sunday scan re-reads Monday's
    wakeup premise.
    — evidence: three hand-built date errors (Aug); #41 UNVERIFIED; wakeup
    #55 on Baghaei's line — Oman's postponement landed 19:37Z Sunday.

17. **A falsifier is scoreable from one named basis, outside the readings'
    disagreement, never on an intraday value.** COMEX settle via relay OR
    the GC_CLOSE row read next morning; `^TNX` row OR wrap close; a proxy
    names the series and the basis. The line sits outside the two readings'
    gap (~25 pts GC, ~5bp 10y, one basis-width for a proxy). A scan that sees
    a breach on the other basis marks "breach trajectory" and leaves the
    score to the brief. AIS/adversary telemetry carries a validity check.
    — evidence: `599d9586` UNCLEAR; `a1fc647f` UNCLEAR (GC dark, line inside
    the basis error); `542f76be` by 0.6bp with the wrap below; `f8b7b030`
    scored at 19:00Z on 4402.9 that finalised 4382.7 (todo-187).

18. **Align the horizon with the claim-text window; list closing claims by
    window-close timestamp.** `created_at + horizon` lands at or after the
    window close; a claim whose window closed before the run is scored in
    that run whether or not `due` prints it; a no-event claim scores only at
    window close. todo-182.
    — evidence: `8babc60f` early (28 Aug); 31 Aug "score ALL 8"; 12 Sep brief
    listed `110d5daa`/`e1e9ba8f` for the wrong day; `a06ae5c5` scored from
    window close before the horizon tick, correctly.

19. **A threshold or level claim spanning a Fed appearance, an FOMC, or a
    tier-1 print lists it as a breaker and caps — and an oil shock is a
    daily unscheduled repricer of the same size.** Fed-pricing thresholds
    across a governor/chair appearance cap at 0.55 unless the speaker's lean
    is named as consistent; USD/yield levels spanning an FOMC/presser/CPI/NFP
    cap at 0.5 unless ≥1.5× range from spot *including the event day's
    move*; while Brent is up ≥5 straight sessions or a UKMTO-confirmed
    enforcement change sits in the window, a threshold with <15 pts of room
    over >3 sessions caps at 0.5; a bracket pair is one band claim.
    — evidence: 0/3 Aug on tone; `41aec282` (50 → 70 with no speaker, on
    NFP/PPI/crude), `09cb81a7` (1.3× range, FOMC inside) missed; `a2171cad`
    `e694f252` `78317918` with the impulse, hit.

20. **A hold line sits ≥ ~1.5× the window's expected range (daily ATR ×
    √days) from spot, and with the impulse it starts at 0.65.** Below that
    distance it is not written above 0.55; FX-pillar claims enumerate both
    central banks' channels.
    — evidence: `b6450c7a` miss; floors 8/8 this regime at an average 0.58
    (the calibration gap); `66db8e8e` held by 4 pts on an oil-UP session.

21. **The next act is not the last act's class.** "At least one more X of
    class C inside ≤7 days", or "actor changes class", caps at 0.5 unless a
    stated policy trigger or scheduled gate for that class is named; the
    default is the structural negative on the current class or the
    class-agnostic version.
    — evidence: `81564330` (Iran rotated bases → warships → hulls after each
    US counter), `c4ef5615` (US stayed tanker-for-tanker fifteen days)
    missed; `c2eafbf8` `bd825267` wording-only; hull-class negatives
    `03627b6b` `e1e9ba8f` `87e409a7` 3/3.

22. **The regime chain is oil → 2y → DXY → gold; kinetic and foreign-CB legs
    are conditional on it.** An escalation reaches gold as a hike and a
    margin call, not a haven bid: a kinetic gap-up or "≥X in session one"
    claim is not written above 0.5 while hike odds are ≥50% (write the
    band); a yen or foreign-CB leg states the DXY move it requires (≥0.3 or
    a cross of 100) or is subordinate to the oil/long-end channel; a named
    risk that has printed ≥3 times without moving gold against the claim
    leaves that family's risk list, replaced by the channel that did move it.
    — evidence: `85f08a28` miss / `58b7daab` hit (fourth kinetic weekend
    with no gap; `ac946837` the fifth); `52e1b990` `54b6127f` hits; twelve
    non-transmission sessions 2–18 Sep; BoJ 18 Sep (USDJPY +0.6%, DXY
    +0.07, gold +1.2% on Brent −2.5%); 10 Sep hawkish ECB, EUR down.

23. **Every re-weight flip carries a tape clause, read from the trigger's
    first print.** Kinetic, rates-data and Fed-event flips alike: the named
    trigger AND GC in the flip's direction from the trigger's first print to
    that session's Globex close (the next settle if it prints after
    17:30Z). A Fed-event flip is written on the next-meeting odds sentence
    at the wrap plus print-to-Globex-close, never on statement wording. A
    flip that fires on the letter but fails on the sequence moves the weight
    at most 5 pts, recorded "trigger printed, target failed."
    — evidence: 9 Sep (second CENTCOM tanker strike printed, gold −1.5%);
    11 Sep CPI (numbers fired, gold up); 11 Sep East-West pipeline (+1.14%
    set before the confirmation, −20 pts after); 16 Sep FOMC (no guidance
    sentence, presser delivered the hard branch; the 17:30Z settle preceded
    the 18:00Z trigger).

## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact — and a posture fact the
   same day.** No geopolitical claim enters `stance.md` without an
   extracted source confirming *who is doing what to whom* and *since
   when*. A headline pairing a US-force actor with withdraw / exit / pull
   out / deploy / carrier is a posture fact the brief extracts in its own
   run; the lede generator is not a who-did-what check. "Reports" with no
   Tehran or Washington statement stay unconfirmed in the text.
   — evidence: 31 Jul Hormuz inverted; 5 Sep Mehr-only Kharg strike; 23 Sep
   Cape Dao; 30 Sep–1 Oct Iraq withdrawal unextracted through a brief and
   seven scans; 1 Oct "tanker crew killed" was Somali piracy; 15 Oct IRGC
   base-evacuation "reports" held unconfirmed (`23a83bcb` HIT).

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class
   named — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Kinetic triggers
   name the actor and the target class. When the falsifier prints, kill the
   call in the same run; when a move prices a call, mark it realized.
   — evidence: 1 Aug, 5 Aug, 31 Aug kills; `c4ef5615` scored as written
   (10 Sep); `a270f3f1` drifted to UNCLEAR when its trigger became
   unreachable (26 Sep).

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — per session, not
   per regime — and "none of the above" is the positioning read.** Repeated
   non-response to a textbook driver identifies the marginal desk; "rates
   desk in control" read as "range-bound" is the error; a setter named last
   week is re-earned this week; oil sets the *size* of a session the 2y
   signs.
   — evidence: `f7d39052` `394dfea7` `031e3470` caps vs `52e1b990`
   `54b6127f` `8e425cab` hits; fifteen oil-UP sessions 2–24 Sep closed gold
   flat/down; 28 Sep–2 Oct gold = f(2y) 5/5; 5–9 Oct the setter changed

5. **A cap is written against the controlling impulse only under three
   conditions, and a gold cap tops at 0.6 even then.** The impulse points
   against the level AND no Fed speaker sits in the window AND the
   in-window data consensus skews with the cap; else 0.5. Any cap inside
   one expected range after an event-driven day, or inside an enforcement
   campaign with <1 ATR of room, tops at 0.5; a "premium, not barrels" cap
   names the enforcement change or the live stalemate statement as its
   first breaker. Prefer the hold-side claim.

   no 10y ≥5.35 before CPI) hit at 0.6 with the impulse.

## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact — and a posture fact the
   same day.** No geopolitical claim enters `stance.md` without an extracted
   source saying who did what to whom, since when. A US-force actor paired
   with withdraw / deploy / carrier / munitions is a posture fact the brief
   extracts in its own run; "reports" with no Tehran or Washington statement
   stay unconfirmed; a principal's "we can do that in 10 minutes" is intent.
   — evidence: 2–3 Nov Qeshm held as sources until Trump's on-record text
   (`b4938377`); 14–15 Nov seventeen IRGC/Army mine claims held as claims;
   21–22 Nov Bahrain — the Interior Ministry text extracted before the class
   entered the stance, Sitra/Bapco left "witnesses only", Fars' two-bases
   claim held.

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class named
   — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Falsifier prints → kill
   the call in the same run; a move prices the call → mark it realized; an
   intent statement neither kills nor scores a claim written on the act.
   — evidence: `a270f3f1` drifted to UNCLEAR; `b4938377` held through
   Trump's 31 Oct intent and scored MISS the run the text printed (4 Nov);
   19 Nov the pre-written f(2y) demotion taken the run its line fired.

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — per session, not
   per regime — and "none of the above" is the positioning read.** A setter
   named last week is re-earned this week; two price-setter sessions in a
   row demote the standing function to a hypothesis; oil sets the size of a
   session the 2y signs; a de-grossing sells gold with every input positive.
   Weights measure the regime, the setter is named beside them per session;
   a scenario that holds the plurality while setting no session is moved by
   rule 21's setter-failure line, not by prose.
   — evidence: Fri 6 Nov 2y held 73%, DXY +0.72%, gold +37 (`80274b64`
   MISS) — a positioning bid; 16–20 Nov five positioning sessions — 2y
   −17.8bp, Brent −4%, the kinetic record worse daily, gold −8.3%, with
   bearish-rates at 40 the plurality and setting none of them.

4. **Chokepoint and shipping claims go through the maritime trade press —
   gcaptain search (todo-005), Maritime Executive, Gulf News/AFP third for
   the Sat–Sun time-late check; a gcaptain extract is trusted only when its
   title matches the feed headline; a hull is counted by vessel name or
   UKMTO warning number, never by headline.** Date-check mandatory on `?s=`
   results; on a title mismatch read the `?s=` index lede for that headline
   before classifying and never score a class on the mismatched extract; a
   warning-count claim says "as carried by" the named outlets; a casualty
   figure stays with the hull its source names.
   — evidence: 8 Nov `?s=` top UKMTO item date-checked to mid-Oct; 14 Nov
   `--fresh` returned Friday's LNG article for the mine headline; 19 Nov the
   scan's headline list counted TARA GAS twice ("LPG tanker abandoned" /
   "TARA GAS burning") and gave it the ADNOC vessel's "11 injured"; 21 Nov
   two UKMTO Khasab reports carried as "possibly one vessel".

5. **A cap against the controlling impulse needs three conditions and tops
   at 0.6 even then.** Impulse against the level AND no Fed speaker in the
   window AND the in-window data consensus skewed with the cap; else 0.5.
   Inside one expected range after an event-driven day, or inside an
   enforcement campaign with <1 ATR of room, 0.5. Prefer the hold-side claim.
   — evidence: `a3ad30b1` hit at 0.6 under all three; `f8b7b030` `65cd057e`
   `09cb81a7` missed; against-impulse 4/17 lifetime, none since 25 Oct.

6. **"Only X can cause Y" must enumerate and reject the third channel —
   named per claim family, in a scan's claim text as much as the brief's.**
   Fiscal/debasement for gold; tone without guidance for Fed pricing; the
   other central bank for FX pillars; the oil leg for "hot print → gold
   down"; the credit leg for "hot print → 2y up" and "oil up → 2y up"; the
   long end for "Fed hold → gold"; the drain (a mediator text) for any
   oil-up claim; the equity margin call for "2y down → gold up" and
   "kinetic worse → gold up". Can't reject it, drop "only."
   — evidence: tone without guidance 7/7; 15 Oct core PPI 0.7 with the 2y

   scan's claim; 16–20 Nov the 2y −17.8bp and a new kinetic class each day

7. **A conditional's trigger is sized like a target or is a scheduled gate;
   a chain test needs a scheduled driver that moves the CURVE, not one
   tenor; a band on a chain input is sized on that input's MEASURED range.**
   A trigger not expected inside the window at ≥1× its own ATR-days becomes
   a band or a sign claim on the chain's input; the form that never goes
   UNCLEAR is the gate certain to print, scored in-session from one named
   basis; never a chain test for the session before a tier-1 print (rule
   20). On an auction gate the driver counts only if the 2y and 10y close
   in the same sign by ≥0.5 ATR each; a 2y-up / 10y-down session is "supply
   split" and UNCLEAR — or the claim is written on the auction tail itself
   (grade ≤C → 2y close above WI). A band on the 2y, Brent, USDJPY or the
   30y is sized on its 10-session realized range with ≥2× the window's
   expected range on the side of any live or dormant stance leg.
   — evidence: three conditionals UNCLEAR by construction (Sep–Oct); gates
   5/5; `ddf6e395` HIT inside the measured range; `db5c80e2` MISS on a C−
   2y auction with the 10y/30y down.

   wrong-reason hit is discounted; a hit inside the measurement error or a
   sign claim at zero distance from its comparator carries nothing; a
   trigger missing by <0.5 ATR with the target running against is

   — evidence: `62c34dbd` letter-only; `6754924a` row held 3890 inside the
   readings gap, settle through; `b4938377` — substance 26 hours before the

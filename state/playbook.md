## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact.** No geopolitical claim
   enters `stance.md` without extracting a source and confirming *who is
   doing what to whom* and *since when* — the two fields headlines most
   often invert or omit.
   — evidence: 31 Jul Hormuz who-blockades-whom inverted; 5 Sep Mehr-only
   Kharg strike; 11 Sep Hormuz-plan report; 23 Sep Cape Dao held "unknown
   projectile" (UKMTO) while relays wrote "Iranian".

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class
   named — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Kinetic triggers
   name the actor and the target class (a hull at the Kharg anchorage is a
   hull, not territory). When the falsifier prints, kill the call in the
   same run; when a move prices a call, mark it realized the same run.
   — evidence: 1 Aug, 5 Aug, 31 Aug kills; 6 Sep flip line rewritten;
   `c4ef5615` scored as written (hull, 10 Sep); `a270f3f1` drifted to
   UNCLEAR the run its trigger became unreachable (26 Sep).

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — not the
   direction.** Repeated non-response to a textbook driver identifies the
   marginal desk; "rates desk in control" read as "range-bound" is the error.
   — evidence: `f7d39052` `394dfea7` `031e3470` caps vs `52e1b990`
   `54b6127f` `8e425cab` (chain named) hits; fifteen oil-UP sessions 2–24
   Sep closed gold flat/down.

4. **Chokepoint and shipping claims go through the maritime trade press —
   and, while todo-005 is open, through gcaptain search, not just ingest.**
   Date-check mandatory on `?s=` results.
   — evidence: 2 Aug; 19 Aug Wafa (todo-005); `bb86ccc9`; `f04926f1`
   `2fbc22dd` 13 Sep; `2df323fa` on gcaptain's 24 Sep Bahrain no-tolls call.

5. **A cap is written against the controlling impulse only under three
   conditions, and a gold cap tops at 0.6 even then.** The impulse points
   against the level AND no Fed speaker sits in the window AND the
   in-window data consensus skews with the cap; else the ceiling is 0.5.
   Any cap inside one expected range (ATR × √days) after an event-driven
   day, or inside an enforcement campaign with <1 ATR of room, tops at 0.5;
   a "premium, not barrels" cap names the enforcement change or the live
   stalemate statement as its first breaker. Prefer the hold-side claim.
   — evidence: `a3ad30b1` hit at 0.6 under all three; `f8b7b030`
   `65cd057e` `09cb81a7` missed; `f5fd7c62` hit at 0.5 with the impulse
   (small information); `f7a0c23d` Brent <103 at 0.55 inside 1× range,
   Rezaei's stalemate line fired it in 18h. Against-impulse caps 0/2 this
   week, 4/15 lifetime.

6. **"Only X can cause Y" must enumerate and reject the third channel —
   named per claim family.** Fiscal/debasement for gold; *tone without
   guidance* for Fed pricing; the *other* central bank for FX pillars; the
   *oil leg* for every "hot print → gold down" line. Can't reject it, drop
   "only."
   — evidence: `394dfea7` `98bbfd33` `b6450c7a`; tone without guidance 4/4
   for ≥10-pt Fed repricings (16 Sep); 11 Sep core 0.318 fired the flip,
   gold +1.4% on oil.

7. **Announcement-timing, framing, and "scheduled event happens" claims cap
   at ≤0.5 unless anchored to a host- or neutral-published schedule.** A
   participant's announcement is not a schedule; coverage volume never
   raises it.
   — evidence: 0/7 lifetime (`7fcb559d` `fb90abae` `df9a603b` `cd49c313`
   `55412a56` `8babc60f` `6c6ed87f`).

8. **No-incident and no-talks claims require a live feed and a falsifier
   the feed can resolve — format and outlet named in the falsifier
   sentence.** If UKMTO is dark, scope to operator/press-confirmed events;
   the text states whether "fired upon" counts and names the source that
   resolves position; an infrastructure claim names the whole chain or
   states node scope. A talks-negative says in the falsifier sentence which
   format scores (any acknowledged round / direct only / a joint text) and
   which Iranian outlet is the government.
   — evidence: `aba1ae09` miss; `6b3ad492` UNCLEAR; `f04926f1` node held
   while the chain was cut; `645cc78a` MISS — body said "direct, both
   governments", falsifier said "resumption"; both confirmed a round and
   disagreed only on the format (23 Sep).

9. **Separate trigger from target in compound claims.** A level-plus-
   mechanism claim is scored on both; a wrong-reason hit is discounted, not
   banked; a hit inside the measurement error carries no information; a
   floor that holds on the row while the tick breaches is a basis-hit,
   named as such.
   — evidence: `7bca8d8f` `6f772055`; floors `746afbe2` `cdb1709a`
   `66db8e8e` `63bf960c` with the buyer unobserved; `717f560c` row held by
   9.5 while the tick went 17 through 4300; `b06eaec0` by 1.6; `f5fd7c62`
   with 1.8% of room.

10. **Skeptic's edge is a floor: structural-negative claims start at 0.8 —
    except across a scheduled gate with both principals present, where the
    ceiling is 0.7 and the format is named (rule 8).** "No co-confirmed
    text / no strike on class X / regime persists" backed by on-record
    positions is written at 0.8+; lower requires naming the gate. The
    brief's 0.7 ceiling (todo-007) still blocks it — name the compliant
    floor in the text.
    — evidence: 57/58 across 3 Aug–26 Sep; 19/20 at the ceiling 7–27 Sep;
    the one miss `645cc78a` fell at UNGA with Witkoff and Araghchi in one
    city — the gate broke, not the position.

11. **Pattern extrapolation needs a live transmission mechanism; a horizon
    >14 days re-verifies it at the midpoint; a reaction-function line ("X
    does not follow Y") is a pattern claim.** Name what carries the pattern
    forward (else cap 0.6); kill at the midpoint if it died. A reaction-
    function observation enters the stance as fact only after three sessions
    in the same direction with the driver moving ≥1 daily ATR; before that it
    is a hypothesis with a named test claim, and both branches of a gate are
    written with the sign the chain gives them.
    — evidence: `9a757c1a` `71096269` misses; `a64b23c1` `bb86ccc9` hits;
    `951d8286`; `ec71ce01` (41 days, midpoint never run); 22 Sep "the front
    end does not follow oil down" (one session, a 2y auction inside it)
    shaped the deal-branch sign until 25 Sep ran the chain textbook.

12. **Operator confirmation stands alone; namelessness downgrades detail,
    not the event; adversary claims are never a trigger.** Incident-count
    and attribution claims score on UKMTO/operator/host-state/US-official
    statements only.
    — evidence: `0aaec9a2` hit, `71096269` miss; `c2eafbf8` `bd825267`
    "or IRGC-claimed" hits carrying zero information; `71bd8ddb`;
    `dd31b40f` held — no US attribution of the Cape Dao through 25 Sep.

13. **Status claims come from running the check — levels, events,
    infrastructure, trajectories, feed gaps and run slots alike.** A
    trajectory call sweeps the claim's whole window from `created_at` and
    cites the earliest qualifying event; a component or feed reported missing
    must have been queried, missing, in the same run; a task handed to a run
    slot names a slot that exists in `agent_runs`.
    — evidence: calendar "outage" ×4 retros; "`bars` empty" (6 Sep); 6 Sep
    trajectory from the latest event only; 11 Sep "no feed carried" (it
    had); 26 Sep stance handed Monday's premise to "the 21:00Z Sunday scan"
    — scans run 05–19Z only, none at 21Z (todo-189).

14. **Data-event runs: schedule off the US print (print+45), never the
    foreign presser; read the print post before the fronts; name the
    component; when a print sits inside 60 min of a Fed speaker, say which
    event owns the odds trigger.** The investinglive print post carries the
    odds sentence first, actionforex's front at ~print+50 second; "no relay"
    is declared only after both. A hot/soft verdict names the largest single
    component and the ex-that number before the odds sentence. The odds
    sentence is read post-print and post-speech (the wrap as tiebreaker);
    the speech test is three-way (further hikes / pause / no content — a
    meeting named is a bonus); an event run that opens a tape clause books
    its own close-out wakeup at Globex close +30. A run that has written
    state never exits `empty` (todo-180).
    — evidence: 12 Aug CPI empty; 4 Sep NFP `empty` after writes; #53; #26;
    #59 Barr — the ≥60% relay was in the flash-PMI post 14 min before he
    spoke, his post carried no odds sentence; #60 closed the flip.

15. **Date agency copy from its own dateline; trust extracts only as of the
    freshness header; check the extracted title against the headline.**
    — evidence: 16 Aug AJ stale index; 19–21 Aug Saree via Mehr; 6 Sep Gulf
    News mis-serve (todo-181); 14 Sep "wounded USAF officer" was April.

16. **A scheduled-date anchor needs primary verification ≤7 days old; a
    diplomatic-event anchor cites the host or venue state; an event landing
    19:00Z–03:30Z has no reader until the brief.** Participant-only
    announcements schedule a scan marker, not a deepdive; the Saturday brief
    re-verifies the coming week; the Monday brief's first act re-reads
    Monday's wakeup premise against the Sunday open; if the desk needs an
    event inside the blind window sooner, the brief books a one-off wakeup.
    — evidence: three hand-built date errors (Aug); #41; wakeup #55 on
    Baghaei's line; 26 Sep 18:09Z on-record Trump rejection unread from the
    silent 19:00Z scan to the 03:30Z brief (todo-189).

17. **A falsifier is scoreable from one named basis, outside the readings'
    disagreement, never on an intraday value.** COMEX settle via relay OR
    the GC_CLOSE row read next morning; `^TNX` row OR wrap close; a proxy
    names the series and the basis. The line sits outside the two readings'
    gap (~25 pts GC, ~5bp 10y, one basis-width for a proxy). A scan that sees
    a breach on the other basis marks "breach trajectory" and leaves the
    score to the brief. AIS/adversary telemetry carries a validity check.
    — evidence: `599d9586` `a1fc647f` UNCLEAR; `542f76be` by 0.6bp;
    `f8b7b030` on a running value (todo-187); `6e168240` bases 2bp apart,
    both clear; `f7a0c23d` by 0.08 on the basis and 4% on substance.

18. **Align the horizon with the claim-text window; list closing claims by
    window-close timestamp; score a determinate outcome when it is
    determined.** `created_at + horizon` lands at or after the window close;
    a claim whose window closed before the run is scored in that run whether
    or not `due` prints it; a no-event claim scores only at window close; a
    claim whose remaining sessions cannot change the outcome scores now.
    todo-182.
    — evidence: `8babc60f` early; 31 Aug "score ALL 8"; 12 Sep wrong-day
    listing; `a06ae5c5`; `1889819a` scored Mon 21 Sep with Tue open — no
    both-up pair was possible; `ac946837` on the first row ≥22:00Z.

19. **A threshold or level claim spanning a Fed appearance, an FOMC, or a
    tier-1 print lists it as a breaker and caps — and an oil shock is a
    daily unscheduled repricer of the same size.** Fed-pricing thresholds
    across a governor/chair appearance cap at 0.55 unless the speaker's lean
    is named as consistent; USD/yield levels spanning an FOMC/presser/CPI/NFP
    cap at 0.5 unless ≥1.5× range from spot *including the event day's
    move*; while Brent is up ≥5 straight sessions or a UKMTO-confirmed
    enforcement change sits in the window, a threshold with <15 pts of room
    over >3 sessions caps at 0.5; a bracket pair is one band claim.
    — evidence: 0/3 Aug on tone; `41aec282` `09cb81a7` missed; `a2171cad`
    `e694f252` `78317918` hit; `1a513d72` (0.55, ≤60% through Barr) missed
    on its named breaker, the flash PMI — a correctly priced miss; band
    partner `4872cd75` hit.

20. **Distance is measured on the instrument's own ATR, and 0.55 is not a
    with-impulse label.** A hold line sits ≥ ~1.5× the window's expected
    range (daily ATR × √days — Brent's and the 2y's as much as gold's) from
    spot; with the impulse it starts at 0.65 there and at 0.6 inside it;
    against the impulse inside 1.5× it is not written above 0.5 (rule 5) —
    write the band. 0.55 is reserved for shapes whose family runs under 70%
    lifetime; FX-pillar claims enumerate both central banks' channels.
    — evidence: `b6450c7a` miss; floors 10/10 this regime, all at 0.55,
    `d571e0f0` `717f560c` at 0.4–0.9× range; `fc5c5a29` hit by 25bp; the
    0.55 bucket 21–27 Sep: with-impulse 8/8, against 0/2 (`f7a0c23d` 4.3%
    of Brent room over three sessions; `1a513d72`); lifetime 0.55 → 80%,
    0.60 → 68%.

21. **The next act is not the last act's class.** "At least one more X of
    class C inside ≤7 days", or "actor changes class", caps at 0.5 unless a
    stated policy trigger or scheduled gate for that class is named; the
    default is the structural negative on the current class or the
    class-agnostic version.
    — evidence: `81564330` `c4ef5615` missed; `c2eafbf8` `bd825267`
    wording-only; class negatives `03627b6b` `e1e9ba8f` `87e409a7`
    `9e629d6e` `d96debd5` 5/5 — US stayed hull/blockade class through
    26 Sep, the Red Sea stayed denial-not-Saudi-hull.

22. **The regime chain is oil → 2y → DXY → gold; kinetic and foreign-CB legs
    are conditional on it, and the drain leg sells gold too.** An
    escalation reaches gold as a hike and a margin call, not a haven bid: a
    kinetic gap-up or "≥X in session one" claim is not written above 0.5
    while hike odds are ≥50% (write the band); a yen or foreign-CB leg
    states the DXY move it requires (≥0.3 or a cross of 100) or is
    subordinate; a named risk that has printed ≥3 times without moving gold
    against the claim leaves the risk list. De-escalation drains the
    premium through the same 2y and has bid gold once in the regime.
    — evidence: `85f08a28` miss / `58b7daab` `ac946837` hits (fifth kinetic
    weekend, no gap); `52e1b990` `54b6127f` `8e425cab` (Brent +3.9% → 2y
    +12bp → DXY +0.5 → gold −1.7%, 23 Sep); 21 Sep Brent −3.7% gold −1.0%;
    25 Sep the first drain-leg bid; theme fit 26 Sep: geopolitics +2.4σ is
    bearish-scored hours followed by gold DOWN (Sep median −0.28%, n=52)
    beside flat bullish-scored hours (n=244) — the drain leg, not a haven.

23. **Every re-weight flip carries a tape clause, read from the FIRST
    trigger's first print, with a one row-step margin.** Kinetic, rates-data
    and Fed-event flips alike: the named trigger AND GC in the flip's
    direction from the first trigger's print to that session's Globex close
    (the next settle if it prints after 17:30Z), by at least ~5 pts / 0.1%.
    When two events share a flip the clause is written against the one that
    owned the odds sentence. A Fed-event flip is written on the next-meeting
    odds sentence at the wrap plus print-to-Globex-close, never on statement
    wording. A flip that fires on the letter but fails on the sequence, or
    closes inside the margin, moves the weight at most 5 pts, recorded
    "trigger printed, target failed / inside the noise."
    — evidence: 9 Sep; 11 Sep CPI; 11 Sep East-West; 16 Sep FOMC; #60
    (23 Sep) — the speech basis closed −1.1 (noise), the PMI basis (odds
    owner, 13:45Z) closed −19 and −1.23% on the session, and decided
    20/50/30 → 15/60/25.

24. **A Fed-speaker slot is booked on venue and title, not on the calendar
    name — and a speech run extracts the Board text first.** The ff rows
    carry the name only; `federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/2026-
    speeches.htm` lists title and venue, the host's event page carries the
    agenda, and the full text posts at `/speech/<lastname><yyyymmdd>a.htm`
    at speaking time. A plumbing / supervision / community venue gets a scan
    marker or a conditional task, never a deepdive slot. A no-policy speech
    produces no relay post at all, so rule 14's relay-first order does not
    apply to speeches.
    — evidence: #57 Jefferson 22 Sep booked on the name — 20 minutes of
    discount-window modernization at the NY Fed Treasury Market Conference,
    no relay post 50 min after; Williams' slot at the same conference was
    reserves-implementation only; #59 Barr was a policy speech and the Board
    text said so before any relay.

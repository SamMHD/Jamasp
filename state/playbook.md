## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact — and a posture fact the
   same day.** No geopolitical claim enters `stance.md` without an extracted
   source saying who did what to whom, since when. A US-force actor paired
   with withdraw / deploy / carrier / munitions is a posture fact the brief
   extracts in its own run; "reports" with no Tehran or Washington statement
   stay unconfirmed; a principal's "we can do that in 10 minutes" is intent.
   — evidence: 31 Jul Hormuz inverted; 30 Sep–1 Oct Iraq withdrawal
   unextracted through eight runs; 15 Oct IRGC "reports" held (`23a83bcb`
   HIT); 2–3 Nov Qeshm — the WSJ-officials report held as sources until
   Trump's on-record confirmation was extracted and scored `b4938377`.

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class named
   — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Falsifier prints → kill
   the call in the same run; a move prices the call → mark it realized; an
   intent statement neither kills nor scores a claim written on the act.
   — evidence: 1, 5, 31 Aug kills; `a270f3f1` drifted to UNCLEAR;
   `b4938377` held through Trump's 31 Oct intent and scored MISS the run
   the on-record text printed (4 Nov); `d5d5d159` scored at the first print.

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — per session, not
   per regime — and "none of the above" is the positioning read.** A setter
   named last week is re-earned this week; two price-setter sessions in a
   row demote the standing function to a hypothesis; oil sets the size of a
   session the 2y signs; a de-grossing sells gold with every input positive.
   — evidence: fifteen oil-UP sessions in Sep closed gold flat/down; 22 Oct
   2y −18.8, gold −2.2%; 29–30 Oct f(2y) demoted after two margin-book
   sessions; Fri 6 Nov the 2y held 73% of its print move and DXY +0.72%
   while gold rose 37 from the print+45 low (`80274b64` MISS) — a
   pre-kinetic-weekend positioning bid, named, not forced.

   gcaptain search (todo-005), Maritime Executive, Gulf News/AFP third for

   at 0.6 even then.** Impulse against the level AND no Fed speaker in the
   window AND the in-window data consensus skewed with the cap; else 0.5.
   Inside one expected range after an event-driven day, or inside an
   enforcement campaign with <1 ATR of room, 0.5. Prefer the hold-side claim.
   — evidence: `a3ad30b1` hit at 0.6 under all three; `f8b7b030` `65cd057e`
   `09cb81a7` missed; against-impulse 4/17 lifetime, none written since 25

6. **"Only X can cause Y" must enumerate and reject the third channel —
   named per claim family, in a scan's claim text as much as the brief's.**

7. **A conditional's trigger is sized like a target or is a scheduled gate;
   a chain test needs a scheduled driver that moves the CURVE, not one
   tenor; a band on a chain input is sized on that input's MEASURED range.**

   split" and UNCLEAR — or the gate claim is written on the auction tail

   or the 30y is sized on its 10-session realized range with ≥2× the
   window's expected range on the side of any live or dormant stance leg.

# Playbook

Earned heuristics only — rewritten weekly by /retro, never by other runs.
Cap: 25 heuristics, one page. Each carries an evidence note.

## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact.** No geopolitical claim
   enters `stance.md` without extracting a source and confirming *who is
   doing what to whom* and *since when* — direction of action and start
   date are the two fields headlines most often invert or omit, and both
   change the gold read.
   — evidence: 30 Jul GDP/PCE listed as upcoming after it had printed;
   31 Jul "Iran is stopping ships in Hormuz" inverted (US blockade of
   Iranian ports since 14 Jul); both corrected only after extraction.

2. **A realized call is spent — don't re-bank it.** When a stance item is
   priced by a move, mark it realized and cut its forward weight in the
   same run.
   — evidence: "hawkish dissent underpriced" (31 Jul) paid 1 Aug (10y
   4.745%, gold −1.5%) and was retired rather than carried.

3. **Gold ignoring its own news is a signal about the price-setter.**
   Repeated non-response to a textbook driver identifies the marginal
   price-setter; check whether that makes unpriced catalysts more
   asymmetric rather than less relevant.
   — evidence: 30–31 Jul — record momentum crash absorbed with no selling,
   then credible strike reports with no buying → "rates desk has the
   wheel" framing for the 3 Aug reopen. Predictive value under test
   (reopen predictions score 3–4 Aug).

4. **Chokepoint and shipping claims go through the maritime trade press.**
   For any Hormuz/chokepoint/vessel claim, check a maritime trade source
   (gcaptain extracts cleanly, carries licensed Bloomberg/Reuters) before
   concluding from general news — general feeds under-cover the maritime
   leg.
   — evidence: 2 Aug — gcaptain resolved who-restricts-Hormuz in one pass
   (CENTCOM counts, IRGC "unauthorized route" admission, Treasury
   designations) after four days of Gulf-press ambiguity; the feed had
   missed the 28 Jul Jordan and 29 Jul Damietta strikes entirely.

5. **Write the flip condition with the call, then honor it.** Every
   directional call carries its falsifier at creation; when the falsifier
   prints, kill the call in the same run, without renegotiation.
   — evidence: 1 Aug gap-up call carried "bearish flip: credible
   de-escalation Sunday"; the 2 Aug pause triggered it and the call was
   declared dead the same morning, pre-maturity.

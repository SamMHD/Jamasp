## Heuristics

1. **Extract before asserting a geopolitical fact — and a posture fact the
   same day.** No geopolitical claim enters `stance.md` without an extracted
   source saying who did what to whom, since when. A US-force actor paired
   with withdraw / deploy / carrier / munitions is a posture fact the brief
   extracts in its own run; "reports" with no Tehran or Washington statement
   stay unconfirmed in the text.
   — evidence: 31 Jul Hormuz inverted; 30 Sep–1 Oct Iraq withdrawal
   unextracted through eight runs; 15 Oct IRGC "reports" held (`23a83bcb`
   HIT); 22–24 Oct NYT/WSJ strike relays held as posture (`df0e8755` HIT).

2. **Write the flip condition with the call — actor and target class named
   — then honor it, and retire a realized call.** Falsifier prints → kill
   the call in the same run; a move prices the call → mark it realized.
   — evidence: 1, 5, 31 Aug kills; `c4ef5615` scored as written; `a270f3f1`
   drifted to UNCLEAR when its trigger became unreachable.

3. **Gold ignoring its own news names the price-setter — per session, not
   per regime — and "none of the above" is the positioning read.** A setter
   named last week is re-earned this week; oil sets the size of a session
   the 2y signs; a de-grossing sells gold with every macro input positive.
   — evidence: fifteen oil-UP sessions in Sep closed gold flat/down; 28
   Sep–2 Oct f(2y) 5/5; 22 Oct 2y −18.8, DXY down, December −16 pts and gold
   −2.2% on the settle, under 4000 in Asia — the margin book set the price.

4. **Chokepoint and shipping claims go through the maritime trade press —
   gcaptain search (todo-005), Maritime Executive, Gulf News/AFP third for
   the Sat–Sun time-late check.** Date-check mandatory on `?s=` results; a
   warning-count claim says "as carried by" the named outlets.
   — evidence: 19 Aug Wafa; 2 Oct second Hormuz hit ~10h late via AFP; 21
   Oct Al-Faw via Mehr 12:07Z and MarEx the same day (`55f22cae` MISS).

5. **A cap against the controlling impulse needs three conditions and tops
   at 0.6 even then.** Impulse against the level AND no Fed speaker in the
   window AND the in-window data consensus skewed with the cap; else 0.5.
   Inside one expected range after an event-driven day, or inside an
   enforcement campaign with <1 ATR of room, 0.5. Prefer the hold-side claim.
   — evidence: `a3ad30b1` hit at 0.6 under all three; `f8b7b030` `65cd057e`
   `09cb81a7` missed; against-impulse 4/17 lifetime; `f11f1021` `3be2f207`
   hit at 0.6 with the impulse.

6. **"Only X can cause Y" must enumerate and reject the third channel —
   named per claim family.** Fiscal/debasement for gold; tone without
   guidance for Fed pricing; the other central bank for FX pillars; the oil
   leg for "hot print → gold down"; the credit leg for "hot print → 2y up"
   and "oil up → 2y up". Can't reject it, drop "only."
   — evidence: tone without guidance 4/4 for ≥10-pt repricings; 15 Oct core
   PPI 0.7 with the 2y −5bp (`7f2116ce` MISS); 22 Oct Brent +4%, 2y −19bp.

7. **A conditional's trigger is sized like a target or is a scheduled gate;
   a chain test needs a scheduled driver; a band on a chain input is sized
   on that input's MEASURED range.** A trigger not expected inside the
   window at ≥1× its own ATR-days becomes a band or a sign claim on the
   chain's input; the form that never goes UNCLEAR is the gate certain to
   print, scored in-session from one named basis; never a chain test for the
   session before a tier-1 print (rule 20). A band on the 2y, Brent or the
   30y is sized on its 10-session realized range — never a nominal ATR from
   memory — with ≥2× the window's expected range on the side of any live or
   dormant stance leg; otherwise the claim waits for the gate.
   — evidence: `a270f3f1` `9e1829f8` `64c7ba41` UNCLEAR by construction;
   auction gates 3/3 (`0a646c8b`); `ddf6e395` offside in 14h — 4.68–4.92
   sized on "~5bp" when the record held 8.7bp over four sessions, then a
   19bp day on the breaker the claim named (scores 28 Oct).

8. **No-incident and no-talks claims need a live feed and a falsifier the
   feed can resolve — format and outlet named in the falsifier sentence.**
   UKMTO dark → scope to operator/press-confirmed events; say whether "fired
   upon" counts; an infrastructure claim names the node; a talks-negative
   names the format that scores and the Iranian outlet that is government.
   — evidence: `aba1ae09` miss; `645cc78a` MISS on "resumption" vs "direct";
   format-named arrangement negatives 5/5 — Rezaei's "insufficient" and
   Araghchi's "still on the table" did not score.

9. **Separate trigger from target in compound claims; score substance
   beside the letter.** A level-plus-mechanism claim scores on both; a
   wrong-reason hit is discounted; a hit inside the measurement error or a
   sign claim at zero distance from its comparator carries nothing; a
   trigger missing by <0.5 ATR with the target running against is
   "substance against", counted in the family's calibration.
   — evidence: `62c34dbd` letter-only; `1e9e6e4a` on a 0bp line; `51f5086d`
   held by 17.9 on the row with the tick through its edge by 0.9;
   `7ea8a0de` hit with the shape's precondition absent.

10. **Skeptic's edge is a floor on the principal's position, not on the
    family's record: structural negatives start at 0.8 — 0.7 across a gate
    with both principals present (format named) — only on a class the actor
    has not reached (rule 12), and the floor is withdrawn the run the
    principal states intent.** The brief's 0.7 ceiling (todo-007) still
    blocks 0.8 — name the compliant floor in the text. When the principal
    (not a proxy, not sources) states intent for the act inside the window,
    the successor is written at the brief's own probability with the hint
    quoted and the family count restarts from it.
    — evidence: 83/86 lifetime through 25 Oct (6/6 this week); misses
    `645cc78a` (UNGA), `5bda2c98` `55f22cae` (locations in a reached class);
    24 Oct Trump "another little Epic Fury" on `df0e8755`'s last day →
    `b4938377` at 0.55.

11. **Pattern extrapolation needs a live transmission mechanism; a horizon
    >14 days re-verifies at the midpoint; a sign test counts only sessions
    on which its driver moved, settle first, and logs a split by TIME.** A
    reaction function enters the stance as fact only after three sessions in
    the same direction with the driver moving ≥1 daily ATR. The test counts
    a session only when the driver moves ≥0.5 of its own ATR (2y ≥2bp, 10y
    ≥4bp, DXY ≥0.2), names as driver the instrument that moved ≥1 ATR on the
    sessions gold moved ≥0.5 ATR, and is demoted by two flat-driver sessions
    in five. When the 2y's close move and its ~13:30 ET level differ in sign
    the session is "timing split — chain post-settle": counted on the ROW
    with the settle noted; a chain-test claim names the 13:30 ET 2y as a
    second comparator.
    — evidence: `9a757c1a` `71096269` `306d7862` misses; `31da0c7d` HIT;
    chain tests since 11 Oct 1/2/1; f(2y) 12/15 on the settle since 28 Sep —
    21 Oct settle +7.4 at 17:30Z, the 2y 4.768 → 4.827 on the ~18:00Z
    headline, row −12.7 (timing split); 22 Oct the de-grossing exception.

12. **Operator confirmation stands alone; an act is scored by its incident
    date; adversary claims are never a trigger; a negative is a class
    negative only on an unreached class — and no location negative inside a
    reached class is written while the count is rising.** Incident and
    attribution claims score on UKMTO/operator/host-state/US-official
    statements only. While the UKMTO weekly/monthly count is rising, a
    negative on a location inside a reached class is not written at any
    confidence; the brief writes the next-class negative instead (terminal
    suspension or force majeure, an operator-confirmed mine, a US-flagged
    hull). A hull/mine negative is scored HIT only after the UKMTO weekly
    (via gcaptain), gcaptain and Gulf News time-late checks for the last
    two window days; a later confirmation re-opens the score at the retro.
    — evidence: `0aaec9a2` hit / `71096269` miss; UKMTO 144–146 a day late;
    `aa7d1d8f` HIT through four IRGC mine claims (CENTCOM "no mines");
    location bets 0/2 (`5bda2c98` `55f22cae`) at the 0.5 cap, both lost to
    the next hull in the same class with 32 attacks in Oct vs 16 in Sep.

13. **Status claims come from running the check against a source that
    carries the status — levels, events, infrastructure, feed gaps,
    speakers and run slots alike.** A trajectory call sweeps the whole
    window; a component reported missing was queried, missing, in the same
    run; a task handed to a slot names one that exists in `agent_runs` — an
    extraction the brief cannot do is a deepdive wakeup or "unextracted,
    sources-only", never a scan errand; a failed weekly slot is made up by
    a same-day wakeup; a superseded conditional wakeup is cancelled and
    rebooked.
    — evidence: calendar "outage" ×4 retros; 3 Oct "no Fed speaker" — six
    on TE/MarketWatch; 11 Oct retro made up by #72; #71 → #73 on the venue;
    `due` printed 0 for a second week while rule 18 scored 11.

14. **Data-event and speaker runs: schedule off the US print (print+45);
    read the print post, then the fronts; name the component; date the odds
    to its own clause; carry the "no odds sentence" outcome; book the
    close-out on the observed row cadence.** investinglive's print post
    carries the odds sentence first, actionforex ~print+50 second; "no
    relay" only after both. A verdict names the largest component and the
    ex-that number (todo-193). The operative odds figure is the latest dated
    relay sentence; a roundup's is context. A speaker flip-line set names
    the prior dated figure it holds at if no odds sentence prints; the
    relay's gloss is not guidance. A data-event wakeup names "the stance's
    flip lines as of the run" and carries no figure; the close-out is an
    absolute time from the row cadence, "poll until the row prints", never
    `empty` after writing state (todo-180, todo-194).
    — evidence: 12 Aug CPI empty; #65 two figures in one hour; #68 `empty`

    wrap's 27% stayed operative (`1e052124` HIT).

16. **A scheduled-date anchor needs primary verification ≤7 days old; a

    disagree is booked off the LATEST reading +60 and re-read from the relay

17. **A falsifier is scoreable from one named basis, never on an intraday
    value — and the claim names which of two bases it lives on.** The ROW
    (first GC_CLOSE row ≥23:00Z, landing 23:16Z–02:16Z) carries Asia and is
    the basis for bands, reopen rows and the both-up pair — the claims whose
    TEXT is on the row. The SETTLE (CNBC `@GC.1` prev-close, from ~23:00Z)
    carries the US reaction and is the first basis for tape clauses, chain
    tests, print co-triggers and every per-session shape branch, the row
    second; a split reading is a basis-hit on the settle's side; the stance
    names the basis beside each branch. 2y/10y: the CNBC 5:05pm ET Yield
    line, the wrap second ("Yield Prev Close" does not roll over a weekend).
    The line sits outside the readings' gap (~25 pts GC, ≥1bp 2y, ~5bp 10y).
    The reopen row is labelled "reopen", never "Mon"; a D+1 comparator is
    written "the D row, to print ~01:00Z D+1" and re-read from the prices
    table; a moving average is quoted with its basis. A scan seeing a
    breach on the other basis marks "breach trajectory".
    — evidence: `ac495b27` MISS by 1.8 on the row, settle through by 58;
    `1a45f84e` UNCLEAR by 0.3 on the row, settle +16.6; 13 Oct "Mon 4231.6"

22. **The regime chain is oil → 2y → DXY → gold; legs are inputs to the 2y

    drain leg is a band, not a cap.** The brief reading a session names the

    after three sessions on substance and is KILLED the same run by a
    risk-off day that sells gold or a credit scare with yields UP; a risk

    De-escalation drains the premium through the same 2y (3/3 bid gold).
    — evidence: 28 Sep–2 Oct f(2y) 5/5; 13–16 Oct the AI-credit bid through

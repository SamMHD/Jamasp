   "unknown projectile" is now identified as a **missile** and it
   **disabled the vessel's engine room** (investingLive wrap). That is
   the third hit in four days and the first confirmed missile — the
   "attack pattern extends" tell of the bullish tail, printed, though
   still unattributed and not Arbaeen-framed.

3. **Iranian info-channels are amplifying into the window,
   unconfirmed:** Iranian media claims of fresh attacks on US bases in
   Kuwait (only the 1 Aug Boubyan strike is corroborated; nothing new in
   the feed), and an F-35B crash claim from Nour News (SNSC-linked), no
   second source. Treat both as escalation-flavored messaging until
   corroborated.

   Baghdad/Najaf during Arbaeen reads as regional diplomacy at least as

   explicitly **ruled out any second shipping corridor** through the

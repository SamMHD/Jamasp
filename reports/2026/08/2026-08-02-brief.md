**Who restricts transit — resolved, attribution sourced.** Not "owner
choice" as a free preference. Three layers:

1. **Iran restricts with force.** The IRGC's own statement (Reuters, 29 Jul)

3. **Owner reluctance is the volume mechanism, downstream of (1).**

Gold consequence: the war-risk/insurance premium in Gulf flows cannot fully
